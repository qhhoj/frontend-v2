## Whole-box links

When a box (card, row, tile, list item) stands for one destination, the WHOLE box is the click target, never only its title or a small button. One pattern, the stretched link:

- `Card to` / `href` (+ `linkLabel` when it has no `title`): the `title` becomes the real link and covers the card. Hover tint, pointer cursor and a focus ring around the box come with it.
- A box that is not a Card (an `<li>`, a tile): give it the `box-link` class and put one `StretchedLink` (`to` or `href`; the title or an `sr-only` label in the slot) inside it. Same look.
- `DataTable rowTo`: the whole row (and the stacked card) opens the page; the primary cell is the link; the ring goes around the row.
- Other links and buttons inside a `box-link` (a tag chip, "Sửa", a menu) sit above the cover automatically and keep their own action. Never nest a link inside the stretched link.
- It is a real `<a>`: Ctrl/Cmd/middle click opens a new tab, and keyboard and screen readers get one link per box. Do not use an `@click` + `router.push` on a box.

The wrappers in the last column stay only for the call sites that already use them; ESLint rejects a new import. The ledger and the rule: `docs/wrapper-migration.md`.

Colour means status: a chip gets a tone only when the tone says something (success/danger/warning/info, a verdict); time, memory, language and other facts are `neutral`. The status tone names are one vocabulary everywhere (`success`, `warning`, `danger`); `positive`/`caution`/`negative` on `AdminChip`, `StatGrid` and `ProgressBar` are deprecated aliases. The count badge of `Tabs`/`SegmentedControl` is `Chip size="sm"`. A `Notice` with `tone="danger"` is always an alert, so it is for a failure that just happened; a standing warning that is not a failure takes `warning`.

Known gaps: `Notice` has no icon override; a single toolbar toggle is `AppButton pressed` (K2). Not built: `ListRow`, `SidebarLayout`, and the parts marked "next" above.

Motion (`.batch/motion-ux/motion-notes.md`; tokens `--qh-duration-*`, `--qh-ease*` in `tokens.css`, zero under reduced motion and low power, so the end state shows at once): `pressable` (scale .97 on a spring) on boxed buttons; popups (`[data-popup]`) grow from their opener's edge, 150 ms; `Modal` scales from .96 and fades in, 200 ms (no exit motion: it unmounts on close); toasts rise 12 px on a spring, move up with `ease-move`, and leave with a 4 px fade.
