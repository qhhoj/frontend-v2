# Workbench — QHHOJ Design System (Master)

Workbench is the QHHOJ design language since 2026-11-15 (owner pick; it replaced Clay and Glass). Its colours are palette A, **Paper & Ultramarine**. Plan and stills: the redesign build plan (`.batch/redesign/`); tokens, primitives and rollout: `docs/design-language.md`. Page overrides go in `pages/<page>.md`; they win over this file.

Source of truth for values is the code: `src/theme/tokens.css` (sizes, radii, type, motion and roles derived from palette tokens) and `src/theme/palette/*.css` (every colour), generated by `npm run palette` from `scripts/palette/paper.mjs`. This file explains intent. Bit, the pixel mascot (`mascot.md`), keeps its sprite colours in every look.

## Style

- Warm paper ground, opaque near-white cards with a 1 px `--qh-line` hairline, small radii, no blur, no blobs, no glow, no gradients on controls (`bg-gradient-accent` is the flat accent). Depth is hairlines and tone steps.
- Dense: 13 px body, 32 px rows (`--qh-row`), 36 px board rows (`--qh-row-dense`), a 224 px side rail (`--qh-rail`). The shell, table and kit steps of the build plan use them.
- Components never know the look: they use `glass rounded-card` (now an opaque card), `bg-surface`, `bg-accent`, `text-muted`; the palette decides what those render.

## The look: mode, contrast, accent, background

- **Mode** `html[data-theme]`: `light` (default when the device says nothing) or `dark`; the preference may be `auto`. Saved as the account's `siteTheme`. Retired ids (`clay-*`, `glass-*`) are read as their successor by the pre-paint script, the stores and the backend.
- **Contrast** `html[data-contrast="high"]`: text, control borders, the accent and the verdict lanes at WCAG AAA-like floors (the palette's file in `palette/`). Without a saved look it follows `prefers-contrast: more`.
- **Palette** `html[data-palette]`: Catppuccin (Latte and Mocha, default and without the attribute), Catppuccin Frappé, Palletism, Wonderlist, Motionin, Iris, Lagoon, Lotus, Ink, Plum, Petrol, Paper (`palette/<id>.css`, chosen on the Appearance page, kept in this browser).
- **Accent** `html[data-accent]`: ultramarine, teal, plum (default), terracotta, graphite; each palette gives them its own hues and names its default (`palette/<id>.css`, one rule per mode × contrast × accent, also on any `[data-accent]` element for swatches). The eight Clay accents map to the nearest new one. Verdicts, first-solve, tiers, coins, tags and syntax never follow the accent.
- **Page background** `html[data-background]`: None (default) or one of seven styles (`theme/backgrounds.css`). The surface is a plain colour with no grid.
- A user whose stored Clay or Glass choice was moved sees one toast pointing to Settings › Appearance.

## Colour roles (light / dark, standard contrast, ultramarine)

| Role | Token | light | dark |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ground | `--qh-ground` | `#f6f1e7` | `#15120f` |
| Card | `--qh-surface` | `#fffefa` | `#1e1a16` |
| Inner tile / input | `--qh-surface-strong` | `#eee7d9` | `#28231d` |
| Hover row | `--qh-surface-hover` | `#e8dfcc` | `#312b24` |
| Hairline | `--qh-line` | `#e0d6c3` | `#3a332a` |
| Text / secondary / muted | `--qh-text`, `--qh-text-secondary`, `--qh-text-muted` | `#1d1a14`, `#423e38`, `#5b5750` | `#e8e4dc`, `#c2bfb6`, `#aca8a0` |
| Accent as text/link | `--qh-accent-text` | `#1d49d6` | `#7aa3fe` |

The generator solves every value against its floor (body text 4.5:1 standard, 7:1 high, also on every accent tint and Blueprint grid line; graphics 3:1 / 4.5:1; labels on fills 4.5:1 / 7:1) and `npm run lint:palette` proves all 20 sets (2 modes × 2 contrasts × 5 accents). Verdict lanes (AC, WA, TLE, MLE, RTE, CE, IE) each have text, tint, solid and an `-on` label; solids are chosen to stay apart under colour-blindness simulation. `src/theme/themes.node.spec.ts` checks the composed cascade and the roles `tokens.css` derives.

## Typography

- **IBM Plex Sans** for UI and headings (`--qh-font-display` is the body face), **IBM Plex Mono** for code and figures with `tabular-nums`. Self-hosted via `@fontsource` with the Vietnamese subset; Sans 400/500/600/700, Mono 400/600.
- Type scale (font size only; leading comes from the primitives): `text-tiny` 11, `text-caption` 12, `text-meta` 13 (also the body size), `text-sm` 14, `text-base` 16 (prose, every input), `text-title` 17, `text-subtitle` 18, `text-section` 20, `text-page` 20 (the `page-title` h1 utility), `text-display` 28, `text-stat` 28 with `font-mono`, `text-hero` 40, `text-score` 40. No arbitrary `text-[Npx]` (`lint:tokens`).

## Shape, depth, motion

- Radius by role: controls `rounded-control` 4, inputs, code and cells `rounded-cell` 4, inset tiles, rows and notices `rounded-row` 6, cards and dialogs `rounded-card` 6 (8 on phones), skeletons 4. `rounded-pill` stays for switches, dots and avatars; chips and tabs leave it in the kit restyle (step 3). A menu or popover is `rounded-row`. No `rounded-[…]` outside the lint allowlist.
- Control heights: `--qh-control` 44 px (md), `--qh-control-sm` 36 px (44 px on coarse pointers; the admin scope's default), `--qh-control-lg` 56 px (swap card submit). Workbench's 28 px desktop control arrives with the kit restyle.
- Z scale: `z-sticky` 10, `z-savebar` 20, `z-header` 30, `z-bit` 40, `z-overlay` 50, `z-toast` 1200 (dialogs and popups are in the top layer, above all of these). No `z-[…]`.
- One soft shadow, `shadow-float` (`--qh-shadow-float`), on floating surfaces only: modals, menus, popovers, toasts. Control borders use `--qh-line-control` (3:1 on the ground); `--qh-line` stays the hairline of cards and dividers.
- Motion: `--qh-duration-fast` 150 ms and `--qh-duration` 200 ms, ease `--qh-ease`, on colour, border and opacity only; no layout-shifting hover. `prefers-reduced-motion`: durations 0, no transitions or animations.

## Loading states

- Skeletons: `SkeletonBlock` in `--qh-skeleton`, pulsing 1.4 s between full and 45 % opacity; static under reduced motion. Shapes: `text` (1em tall, inline), `round`, `pill`, or a plain block; never a radius class.
- Shaped like the real content: a block inside a wrapper that carries the real text classes, fixed sizes for coins, badges and tiles, the real row count. Skeleton rows also reserve phone-only rows that would otherwise shift (the ranking scroll hint below `md`).
- `QueryState` owns the loading branch and shows the card's own `skeleton` slot; the error branch with retry is unchanged. Wrapper and blocks are `aria-hidden`; no loading text per block.
- Waiting means first data only (`isPending`), and only the waits that begin before the page first paints: a section that starts loading after that (comments, a source card) shows its own skeleton, no Bits. A wait is shown after 600 ms of continuous waiting; a wait that ends is over after 100 ms, so the gap between a navigation ending and the page's queries starting does not count as done.
- Cold start: while the first navigation waits for the session, the shell shows the header (a neutral pill where the account controls go, no current nav item) and, on public pages, a page skeleton. No page and none of its data load before the session answers.
- Bit swarm: after the 600 ms, 5 Bits (3 under 768 px) wander the gutters beside the content column, or a band along the bottom of the viewport where a gutter is under 1.5 Bit widths, never over the header or the page: random walk at 40-120 px/s, bouncing off the lane's edges, small hops, leg steps, facing their direction. Fixed, `z-bit`, `aria-hidden`, `pointer-events: none`, below menus and dialogs.
- When waiting ends, the Bits stop where they stand after 100 ms and fade out over 300 ms. A new wait while they fade walks again.
- Under reduced motion one Bit (the cap) stands in the bottom-left corner and disappears without a fade when waiting ends (the global 0 ms transition rule applies).
- Background refetches, polls and kept-data filter changes show neither skeletons nor Bits.
- One polite live region in the shell says the loading text ("Đang tải…") while the Bits walk; it still announces under reduced motion.
- 404: crying Bit in front of a pale "404" built from `--qh-skeleton` blocks; heading "Page not found", one line, "Back to home" (primary) and "Browse problems" (secondary), the requested path in mono.

## Components

- **Card**: `ui/Card.vue` (variant `glass` = `glass rounded-card`; `padding` or caller padding): an opaque hairline card.
- **Pill nav / tabs**: `glass` pill container; active item `bg-accent-soft` + `text-accent-soft-text` + accent border (one active style for every tab set), others `text-muted` with a `bg-surface-hover` hover.
- **Primary button**: `bg-accent` (or the flat `bg-gradient-accent`) + `text-on-accent`, 56 px tall (`h-14`) in the swap card, 44 px elsewhere.
- **Token pill**: pill with a coin (`coin-1` ... `coin-6`) and label; selected = accent border + accent tint.
- **Verdict badge**: 40 px circle (`size-10`), verdict tint, abbreviation + accessible full label.
- **Ranking cell**: `rounded-cell` block; first solve = `bg-first-solve` with a star: a stronger shade of the accepted green in every theme (deeper in the light themes, brighter in the dark ones; `themes.spec.ts` checks 3:1 against the accepted tint, the hue gap from the red wrong-answer tint and 4.5:1 text); pending = `?n`.
- **Overlays** (menus, popovers): opaque `bg-surface-solid`.
- **Appearance panel**: a 44 px palette button in the header opens an opaque popover with Mode (Light, Dark, Automatic), Contrast (Standard, High), five accent dots, low-power, "use site default" and a link to Settings › Appearance. It has a focus trap; Escape returns focus to the button.
- **Settings › Appearance** (`/settings/appearance`, public): Mode, Contrast, five accent swatches, Page background (None + 8, Blueprint default), reset and a live preview.
- Disabled segments, swatches and sliders use a token fill, border and `cursor-not-allowed`, never `opacity`.

## Rules

- No raw colour values outside `src/theme/themes/*.css` (`npm run lint:tokens`). Status colours: `text-warning`/`bg-warning-bg` (the TLE pair) and `text-info`/`bg-info-bg` (the tag-5 pair); verdict colours only for verdicts.
- Text >= 4.5:1 on the worst ground, large text >= 3:1; accent fills and the accent border >= 3:1; focus ring 2 px `--qh-focus-ring`, offset 2 px, never removed.
- Meaning never by colour alone: verdicts carry text, frozen cells carry `?`, first solve carries a legend.
- Touch targets >= 44 px; real `<button>`/`<a>`; icon-only buttons have `aria-label`.
- Icons: Lucide only, no emoji. The one exception: programming-language marks through `LanguageIcon`/`LanguageLabel` (monochrome, in the text colour, beside the language name, never a control).
- Every accent preset keeps the default's contrast rules (`themes.spec.ts`). Disabled controls keep full-contrast labels: no `opacity` on text.
- Responsive at 375, 768, 1024, 1440; wide tables scroll inside their card, never the page.
